Privacy Policy

Last updated: 26 October 2026

The short version

Circle works without an account, and everything you enter stays on your phone. Only if you sign in with Google to use shared groups does Circle store anything on its server, and then only what's needed to share those groups with the people in them. Circle has no ads and no analytics, and doesn't sell or share your data.

Data that stays on your phone

Accounts, transactions, local groups, investments, Zakat calculations and settings are stored only on your device. Circle can't see them. Backups you export are files you control.

Data stored when you use shared groups

When you sign in with Google, Circle receives and stores:

  • Your Google account ID, name, email address and profile picture link, to identify you and show you to people in your groups.
  • Sign-in sessions (a hashed token, the device name and when it was last used), to keep you signed in.
  • The shared groups you create or join: group names, members, expenses, splits, paybacks, notes, approvals and their change history. Everyone in a group can see that group's contents.

Circle doesn't access your contacts, Gmail, Google Drive or anything else in your Google account, and doesn't upload the local data described above.

How it's used

Only to provide shared groups: syncing them between members, sending invites you create, converting currencies and keeping the service secure (for example, limiting how often requests can be made).

Where it's stored

On Cloudflare's servers (Cloudflare Workers and D1), sent over encrypted connections. Google handles sign-in under Google's privacy policy.

Deleting your data

In Circle, open Settings → Delete Circle account. This immediately removes your Google account ID, name, email and profile picture and signs out all your devices. Your entries in shared groups stay with the group so the other members' balances still add up, but they're no longer linked to you: your place becomes a placeholder under the name the group knew you by. Groups with no other members are deleted. You can also email us to ask for deletion.

Children

Circle isn't directed at children under 13, and shared groups shouldn't be used by them.

Changes
